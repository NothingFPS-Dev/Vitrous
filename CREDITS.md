# Image credits

## Rendered in-house
`ESC — Obsidian` is generated by this repo: `lib/glass.ts` builds the geometry
and glass material, `/scenes/[slug]` renders it, and
`scripts/render-products.mjs` captures it to `public/products/`.
Vitrous owns this image outright.
